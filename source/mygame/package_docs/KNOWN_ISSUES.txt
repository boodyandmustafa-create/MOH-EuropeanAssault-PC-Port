KNOWN ISSUES - 0.9.3-beta
=========================

* MAP EDITOR IS EXPERIMENTAL (off by default). It may show bugs, and in rare cases it can crash or freeze the game,
  especially with many placed soldiers or when changing levels. Your edits live in mods\maps\<level>.txt; if a level
  misbehaves, turn the editor off (F1 > Mods and tools) or delete that level's file. Limits: 16 living editor soldiers,
  no prop rotation, no weapons from other levels, zones cannot be edited after placing, zone rings show through walls,
  soldiers can be born inside crates, the drag preview is a stand-in shape, the level's own pickups cannot be removed,
  window text is English only, no launcher row.
* Sound may rarely vanish in long sessions (a stuck software mix buffer). A guard restarts it automatically;
  if the sound is gone for good, save and restart the game, and please attach play_log.txt to a report.
  If a sound fades or vanishes when you turn the camera to one side, open F1 > Audio > Stereo sound and switch it to Mono
  (the game's own mix puts a side sound in one ear only; Mono feeds both ears, but left / right / behind can no longer be told apart).
* If the game crashes: open a GitHub issue and attach crash.txt, crash.dmp and play_log.txt from the game folder (or use
  the launcher's "Report a problem" button, which makes MoH_report.zip). Say how long you had been playing and whether
  you use overlays (RTSS, Discord, Steam, ReShade). Crashes after a long session are still being investigated.
* If parts of the ground turn pure black, press F2 while it is black and attach play_log.txt and the screenshot to an issue.
* 60 to 165 fps run well on the author's PC (RTX 4060). 240 fps and weaker PCs can still stutter in busy scenes;
  pick a lower frame rate and attach play_log.txt (it holds frame-time lines). Busy places can fall below the
  best frame rate, especially with the map editor, a wide view (21:9) or "Weapon lighting" on High / Extreme.
* First run and new areas compile shaders (short stutters); they are cached in shader_cache\.
* "Corpse fix (60 Hz physics)" (off by default) stops bodies flying at high fps but can freeze or crash the game.
* Enemy count (x2 to x5) is heavy: it can cost frames, may not complete a "kill every enemy"
  objective as expected, and can crowd the sound mixer. Keep it low on a slower PC.
* Enemy grenades is on Low by default: enemies who shoot at you sometimes throw a grenade. It has no throw animation
  (the grenade appears at the soldier's hand). Set it to Off in Recomp Settings (F1) > Gameplay if you do not want it.
* Model detail "Higher" / "Highest" draws better models farther away; its frame cost is not measured on every PC.
* The Karabiner fix covers the player's fast bullets. The Karabiner is confirmed; other heavy rifles share its class
  but were not each tested.
* At 30 fps the sniper-scope zoom can look like a cut instead of a smooth transition.
* Enemy accuracy, reaction time and sight range cannot be changed (the game does not expose them).
* The Arabic language is experimental: only part of the text is translated, the rest stays English.
* Needs a GPU with Direct3D 11 (feature level 11.0) and the Visual C++ Redistributable (the launcher tells you if it is missing).
* Soldiers may freeze or respawn doubled when "Keep corpses" (experimental) is on.
* Weapon mods: only magazine size is confirmed; damage, range, fire rate and max ammo are experimental. Sound replacement
  mods are untested end to end. DDS texture replacements: uncompressed, DXT1/3/5, BC4, BC5 and BC7 work; BC6H does not.
* Multiplayer: local split-screen works. There is NO online / LAN play, NO bots and NO campaign co-op: the original
  game has no network code and no bot AI, and the campaign is scripted for one player.
* Windows SmartScreen / antivirus may warn about the unsigned executables (false positive).

Report others here: https://github.com/boodyandmustafa-create/MOH-EuropeanAssault-PC-Port/issues
Please attach play_log.txt (and play_log_prev.txt, crash.txt and crash.dmp if the game crashed).
